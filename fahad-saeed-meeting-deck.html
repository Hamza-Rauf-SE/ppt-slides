Using pretrained models for seizure prediction

The paper turns EEG signals into inputs that vision and language models can use.

Parani, Mohammad, and Saeed - 2025

Pretrained Vision Transformers and LLMs for Epileptic Seizure Prediction

What I learned

  • EEG needs careful reshaping before pretrained models can use it.
  • Only a small set of layers was trained, which reduced training cost.
  • The models were tested on patient-independent benchmarks.

The LLM reached 79.02% average sensitivity, 70.80% accuracy, and 71.40% average AUC.

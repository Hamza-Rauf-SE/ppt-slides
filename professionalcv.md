Numerical analysis of radiative flow in a two-phase Bingham Papanastasiou Rheological
nanofluid model: A comparative study using a Morlet-Based Wavelet Neural Network
Optimization. (In Review)
Authors : Hamza Rauf, Umair Khan, Anuar Ishak
Investigates two-phase radiative nanofluid transport under a zero mass flux boundary condition
to ensure physically consistent nanoparticle behavior. The similarity transformed nonlinear
system is solved using MATLAB bvp4c to generate high fidelity benchmark data for skin friction
coefficient and Nusselt number. Develops a Morlet-based Wavelet Neural Network surrogate
and compares Levenberg Marquardt and Adam optimization strategies, showing LM achieves
near-exact accuracy and enables fast parametric evaluation for thermo-fluidic design and
optimization.

by over 90%, establishing a highly robust surrogate model for complex computational fluid
dynamics.

a powerful method for instilling complex reasoning capabilities in compact LLMs.
